## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Tower types are managed from the staff app

The system SHALL let staff create, edit and retire tower types without leaving the staff app.

#### Scenario: Managing types

- **WHEN** a staff user opens the tower types page
- **THEN** they SHALL be able to list, create, edit and delete types
- **AND** each type SHALL be shown rendered as it will appear on a map, rather than as its icon's name
- **AND** the page SHALL be reachable from the staff navigation
